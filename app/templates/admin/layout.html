THE RAIL ON THE LEFT IS THE WHOLE ARGUMENT Fifteen screens with fifteen destinations do not fit in a horizontal bar, and a bar is what forces the icons-only → tooltip → guessing progression every admin panel goes through once it has more than six sections. A rail can hold all fifteen with their names visible, group them into the five things an operator actually does, and still leave the content column most of the width. The groups are numbered 01–05 because the order is real — see `_nav.py`. THE COUNT ON A RAIL ENTRY IS NOT DECORATION Pages, Participants, Media and Messages carry a live count, and Consent carries the count that matters most: consented but undated, the state §5.6 says nothing else on the panel would mention.
