THE SPLIT IS THE SCREEN Sections down the left (what the reader sees, in order), and a fixed right column holding the two decisions about the page as a whole: whether it is published, and what its address and menu label are. The publish gate lives in that column directly above the button that is blocked by it, because a control that refuses to work must say why next to itself. ORDER IS EDITABLE WITH BUTTONS ↑ and ↓ renumber the whole list, one POST each. §11.2 asks for a keyboard alternative to dragging, and a drag handle is unusable from a keyboard and on a phone. The order number is printed beside every section so the effect of the button is visible before it is pressed. HIDDEN IS NOT DELETED A hidden section is skipped at render (§9.3 rule 4) and keeps everything that was typed into it. Deleting is a separate, deliberately plainer form at the end of the row, and its own flash message says the payload survives in the audit log. INVALID SECTIONS ARE SHOWN, NOT HIDDEN A section whose payload does not validate is listed with the schema's own English reason and is refused at publish.
