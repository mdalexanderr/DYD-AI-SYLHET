{# ============================================================================= admin/participants/edit.html — routes 21, 22 and 23. One screen for a new participant and an existing one. plan.md §11.3, §5.3. WHY CREATE AND DETAIL ARE THE SAME TEMPLATE The fields are identical; only the action changes. Two templates would be two places to add the next field §5 asks for, and the second one would be forgotten. THE THREE FORMS ARE DELIBERATELY SEPARATE Details, consent, and publish. They are different kinds of act — a correction, a legal record, and a decision to make a name public — and each writes its own audit entry. One combined form would make "publish" a side effect of pressing "save", which is exactly the accident §5.3 rule 1 exists to prevent. CONSENT IS RECORDED, NOT TICKED A grant carries a DATE and a SOURCE, and a grant without a date is allowed but blocks publication — that case gets its own explanation rather than being treated as an error, because the paperwork genuinely exists and only the date is missing. WHAT IS DELIBERATELY ABSENT A phone field, an email field, a date-of-birth field, an address field and a mark field. They are not in this form because they are not in the database (§5.1), and a CMS that offered them would be asking an operator to collect data the programme has promised not to hold.
